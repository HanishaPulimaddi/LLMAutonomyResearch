An applicant is eligible if their effective PhD award date is on or after 1 March 2021.

The effective PhD award date is the PhD award date, extended by the total duration of any allowable career interruptions, counted in calendar months.

Only career interruptions that occurred after the PhD was awarded can be counted. Periods that overlap are counted only once.

Allowable career interruptions:

- Unemployment.
- Illness or recovery from a medical condition.
- Caring responsibilities for a dependent family member.
- Parental leave.
- Primary carer of a dependent child: up to two years per dependent child. This period includes any parental leave taken for that child; parental leave within the primary-carer period is not added to it.
- Employment in a non-research position, only where the position was not concurrent with research employment.
- International relocation: up to three months for each international relocation.
- Limited or no access to the facilities and resources required for research.
- Disaster management and recovery.
